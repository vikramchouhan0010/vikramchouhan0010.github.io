Greenhouse structure kits: what to send with an enquiry

BLUSTAL lists tunnel, shade net house, retractable roof, Quonset, gable, hoop tunnel, mist chamber, snow-region and cable net house kits. Send your crop, plot size, district, the kit type or problem (heat, rain, hail, wind, snow), buyer type and quantity, and ask for frame, covering, fixings, foundation, delivery and tax as separate lines.
