Shade nets for greenhouses: what to ask for

Shade net is sold by percentage shade, material and weight. Before ordering, confirm the shade percentage for your crop and season, whether the net is UV-stabilised, the roll width that suits your structure so there are fewer joints, and the edge finish. A wholesale buyer should also ask for a sample and written specification.
