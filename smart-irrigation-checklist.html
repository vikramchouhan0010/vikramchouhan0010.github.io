Smart irrigation controllers: points to check before buying

A smart controller adjusts watering to conditions instead of a fixed timer. Confirm: the number of zones against your layout, which sensors it supports (soil moisture, rain, flow), how it connects (Wi-Fi, GSM or local), whether valves are compatible with its output, and what happens during a power or network failure.
